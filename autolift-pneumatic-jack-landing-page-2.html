How the System Works

Compressed air is directed through the pneumatic control system to the actuator. The pneumatic cylinder converts air pressure into linear force, which is transferred through the mechanical lifting structure to raise the supported vehicle.

01

Connect Air

Connect the jack to a suitable compressed-air supply and regulated pneumatic circuit.

02

Activate Control

The operator activates the electrical control, commanding the solenoid valve.

03

Lift

The pneumatic cylinder generates lifting force through the mechanical jack structure.
